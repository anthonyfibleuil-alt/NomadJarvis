NomadJarvis privacy notice

Updated 12 September 2026

NomadJarvis is an invite-only AI workspace operated by Anthony Fibleuil. Contact anthony.fibleuil@gmail.com about your account or privacy.

Information used

Google sign-in provides your Google account identifier, name and verified email. These identify your account and check whether the owner has allowed access. NomadJarvis does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google content.

Your saved conversations, memories and studio text drafts are stored with your account in Cloudflare. Session cookies keep you signed in; short-lived login cookies help secure sign-in. Request counters help limit abuse.

AI processing

When you use AI features, the content needed for that request is sent to the configured AI provider, OpenAI. Chat requests include relevant conversation history and your saved memories. Image and motion features send the content you submit. Optional web search may use your request to search the web. Provider processing is subject to its own policies.

Account privacy and control

Members cannot view one another's conversations, memories or drafts through the app. The owner manages allowed email addresses and operates the underlying infrastructure, which can technically access stored data. Google and Cloudflare process information needed to provide authentication and hosting.

You can delete saved conversations and memories in the app. Revoking access ends sessions but does not erase stored workspace data. Contact the owner to request account and draft deletion. Data is retained until deleted; backups and provider records may persist under their respective retention settings.
